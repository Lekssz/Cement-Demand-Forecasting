Future weather was identified as a potentially useful predictor, particularly for heavy rainfall, but site-level location information was unavailable. Therefore, weather was excluded from the deployable 8-week model to avoid introducing arbitrary geographic assumptions.

So the forecasting problem is not evenly distributed across the sites. The weak performance is concentrated entirely in aggressive and chaotic sites.
The dataset appears to generate deliveries roughly independently of the site's consumption behavior. Conservative sites consume much less, but their deliveries remain around 30 tonnes/day, causing unused inventory to accumulate continuously because the inventory calculation does not appear to enforce the supplied silo capacity.
